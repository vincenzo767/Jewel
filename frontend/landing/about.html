Heritage

A small shop in Talisay City, a big promise.

Bryle's began in 2021 as a single glass counter in downtown Talisay, where our founder repaired family heirlooms and set the occasional engagement ring for neighbours.

Word travelled the way it does on an island — from mother to daughter, from one wedding to the next. Today our atelier employs fourteen master jewellers, many trained in the Filipino filigree and granulation techniques that are slowly disappearing elsewhere.

We've grown, but the counter is still there. So is the promise we made on the first day: to make each piece as if it were for our own family.

Craftsmanship

From stone to heirloom.

Every Bryle's piece passes through at least eleven pairs of hands and four stages before it reaches yours.

01

Sourcing

Our gemologists hand-select every stone from conflict-free, fully traceable suppliers — and Philippine South Sea pearls from Palawan farms.

02

Design

Each design begins as a pencil sketch in our studio, then a hand-carved wax model, refined until it sits perfectly on the body.

03

Setting

Master setters secure each stone under magnification, raising every prong by hand so the diamond receives as much light as possible.

04

Finishing

Polished, inspected, stamped with our hallmark — and only then wrapped in our signature emerald box, ready to be given.

What we believe

Our values

Ethical sourcing

Kimberley Process–compliant diamonds, recycled gold wherever possible, and full transparency about where every stone comes from.

Uncompromising quality

Solid gold, platinum and sterling silver only. Every piece is hallmarked, inspected three times, and backed by lifetime care.

Living artistry

We train apprentices in traditional Filipino filigree and granulation, keeping centuries-old techniques alive in every collection.
