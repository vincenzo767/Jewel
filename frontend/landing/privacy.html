Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bryle's Diamonds-Gold Jewelry Shop ("Bryle's Diamonds", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit this website, create an account, reserve jewellery or message us. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information we collect

  • Account details you give us when you register: your full name, email address and password. We store only a secure, one-way hash of your password, never the password itself.
  • Profile details you choose to add: phone number, address, a short bio and a profile photo.
  • Reservations: the pieces you reserve, sizes, quantities, your chosen payment method and the status of each reservation.
  • Messages you send us through the website chat, including any piece you attach to a question.
  • Favourites and bag: the pieces you save or add to your bag.
  • Security records: the time of your last sign-in and failed sign-in attempts, used to protect your account from misuse.

If you use the "How far am I?" feature on our Visit Us page, your browser asks for permission to use your location. Your location is used only inside your browser to calculate the distance to our shop. It is never sent to or stored by us.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To hold and prepare the pieces you reserve, and to tell you when they are ready for pickup.
  • To answer your questions and messages.
  • To keep the website and your account secure.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

Cookies

We use only the cookies the website needs to work: a session cookie that keeps you signed in and a security cookie that protects forms from cross-site request forgery. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Services we rely on

Some parts of the website are delivered by other providers, who may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads their content:

  • Google Fonts, for the website's typefaces.
  • Unsplash, for some of the photographs.
  • OpenStreetMap, for the map on our Visit Us page.
  • Our hosting and database providers, who store the website and your account data on our behalf.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is open. Reservation records may be kept for up to five years for accounting and tax purposes, as required by Philippine law. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information unless we are legally required to keep it.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object to processing of, and to have erased the personal information we hold about you. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

How to delete your data

To delete your account and personal information, email hikimurieunwoo@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" from the email address on your account, or message us through the website chat. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

How we protect your information

The website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Passwords are hashed, accounts are locked after repeated failed sign-ins, and only the shop owner can see customer information.

Children

Our website is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
